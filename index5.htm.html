PERFECT GAME

In baseball, a perfect game occurs when a pitcher retires every batter they face in a nine-inning game without allowing any of them to reach base, meaning no hits, walks, or errors. This means the pitcher must not allow any batter to get to first base by any means.
It is an extremely rare and prestigious achievement, rarer than a no-hitter

TRAINING PROGRAMS

ARBITRATION:

After three years of service time, players become eligible for salary arbitration, where they can negotiate their salary with their team. If they can't agree, a neutral party decides the salary.

FREE AGENCY:

After six years of service time, players become free agents and can sign with any team.

PENSION:

Players with at least 10 years of service time earn a full pension, according to the MLBPA, which includes continued medical coverage for themselves and their families after retirement.

SERVICE TIME MANIPULATION:

There have been instances where teams have strategically kept players in the minor leagues to delay their service time, thus delaying their eligibility for arbitration and free agency.
